How to read this:
The left graphic is the grip circle. The right graphic is the same moment on track.

Entry: the car is still braking and starts turning in.
Apex: the car is near the inside of the corner with the most steering angle.
Exit: the wheel unwinds and throttle comes in.

Tight / push: the car runs wide and misses the inside.
Loose / free: the rear rotates too much and the car points inward more than intended.
